The upcoming Twenty One Pilots album, Breach, is set to release on September 12, 2025. It follows the first single, "The Contract," which was released on June 12, 2025. This album is described as the conclusion of a narrative arc that started with their previous album, Clancy, and delves into themes of mental health struggles.

This new album is set to bring to a close the decade-long narratives that began with 2015's Blurryface and continued through records such as 2018's Trench and 2021's Scaled and Icy, referencing the main characters of the lore, Clancy (from the 2024 LP) and Blurryface.

Tyler Joseph and Josh Dun formed Twenty One Pilots in 2009, initially releasing two independent albums. Their major-label debut, 2013's Vessel, marked their commercial breakthrough, eventually peaking at No. 21 on the Billboard 200 chart. The duo achieved their first No. 1 with Blurryface in 2015, and all subsequent releases have consistently reached the top three.

Discography - Twenty One Pilots has released seven studio albums:

The duo are also known for their live albums, EPs, and numerous singles. The band is known for its genre-bending music and conceptual storytelling across their albums, often exploring themes of mental health, self-doubt, and hope.

Self Titled (2009)

"Twenty One Pilots" self-titled debut album is a raw and introspective collection of songs primarily written by Tyler Joseph. It explores themes of faith, doubt, mental health struggles, and the human condition, with a focus on personal experiences and vulnerability.

Regional At Best (2011)

"Regional at Best" is the second studio album by twenty one pilots. This album is significant as it was the final release prior to their signing with Fueled by Ramen, and it marks the first appearance of Josh Dun behind the kit. The album highlights a transition towards more electronic and synth-oriented sounds, merging pop, rock, and alternative genres. Numerous tracks from "Regional at Best" were re-recorded for their follow up album, "Vessel".

Vessel (2013)

"Vessel" represents the third studio album by tøp. It is distinguished by its blend of various musical genres, such as piano-driven indie-rocktronica, and its examination of intricate themes including depression and faith. The title of the album, "Vessel," symbolizes the concept that our physical forms serve as temporary vessels for something of greater significance – our thoughts, messages, and purpose.

Blurryface (2015)

"Blurryface" is the senior studio album from the band. It is a concept album featuring the character Blurryface, portrayed by Tyler Joseph, who symbolizes the insecurities and self-doubt of the frontman. The album delves into themes such as anxiety, depression, and the battle to conquer personal demons. It made history as the first album in the digital era to have every track reach at least a gold certification.

Trench (2018)

"Trench" is a concept album that ventures into themes of mental health, suicide, and uncertainty, set against a fictional backdrop. The album introduces the city of Dema, characterized by oppression, alongside the surrounding region known as Trench, which symbolizes a quest for liberation and self-empowerment. This album was produced following a year-long hiatus and highlights a varied sound, emphasizing atmospheric and mood-driven music.

Scaled And Icy (2021)

"Scaled and Icy" represents the sixth studio album by Twenty One Pilots. This album is noted for its more vibrant and lively sound in contrast to their earlier work, "Trench", and was produced during the COVID-19 pandemic when the band members were collaborating from different locations. The album transitioned into themes of solitude, anxiety, and the quest for hope and creativity amidst a time of significant transformation and unpredictability.

Clancy (2024)

"Clancy," the band's seventh album, marks the conclusion of a multi-album narrative that commenced with "Blurryface". The album showcases a blend of high-energy and melancholic tracks, mirroring the protagonist's journey and the overarching narrative of the album. The album covers the story of Clancy, a character striving to escape the dystopian city of Dema, which symbolizes mental health challenges. "Clancy" examines themes of overcoming personal adversities, rebellion against Dema (which signifies internal conflicts), and the cyclical nature of emotions.
